How to use AV IR

On your phone

  1. Install the app from Google Play and open it. Allow notifications if you want playback controls in the shade. Playback still works if you decline.
  2. Discover lists stations for the country and languages you choose. Set the default country and languages in Settings. Changing the country starts that list fresh. Coming back to the default country brings the saved languages with it. Settings also switches the app between a white theme and a dark theme.
  3. Search by typing a station, city, or genre. Tap the microphone and say the same kind of query. Voice search uses the speech recognizer already on the phone.
  4. Play a result. The station keeps going when you leave the app. Pause, or skip to the next and previous station, from the player bar, the notification, or the lock screen. When the playing station is off screen, the list scrolls it into view.
  5. Save a station with the heart. My Stations keeps those stations, plus the ones you played recently. Drag a saved station to reorder it, or pin it to the top. Next and previous on that tab stay inside My Stations.
  6. Add your own tabs. Open Settings, then Tabs. You can keep five. Give each one a name and a language or search, such as News or Tamil. The tabs sit above Discover. Drag the handle beside a tab to change the order, and use the pencil in Settings to rename it. Next and previous stay in the tab you are listening to, and that list scrolls to the station that starts playing.

In Android Auto

  1. Connect the phone to the car with the cable or wireless Android Auto, the same way you connect any other media app.
  2. Open media and choose AV IR. If it is hidden, open the Android Auto app on the phone, go to Customize launcher, and enable AV IR.
  3. Browse Discover, Search, and My Stations. Your tabs, plus Country and Languages, are under More. The heart on a station saves it.
  4. Skip inside the list you opened. Next and previous on Discover stay in Discover. The same is true for My Stations and for each tab you created. Use the up and down arrows to scroll Discover.
  5. Search by voice while driving. Use the search control in Android Auto, or say “Play jazz on AV IR” to the Google Assistant. The app receives the words, not a recording.
  6. Type when the car is parked. Android Auto shows a keyboard for media search only while the vehicle is parked. The same search runs either way.

If a station will not play

Streams belong to the broadcaster. Some are offline, blocked on mobile networks, or published as an old address. Try another station, or open the same one again. AV IR will try the directory’s backup address once.
