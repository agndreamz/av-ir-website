Phone and Android Auto

Internet radio that stays with you in the car.

Search the Radio Browser directory by voice or by keyboard, save stations, and keep listening when the screen is off. In the car, speak a station while driving. Type when the car is parked.

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Phone

Type or talk

The search field and the microphone are both on the phone. Ask for a station, a city, or a genre such as jazz.

Android Auto

Voice, then keyboard

Android Auto takes voice search while the car is moving. When the car is parked, the same search accepts the keyboard.

Library

Yours to keep

Favorites and recent stations stay on the device and show up in the car. There is no account to create.

Download

Install AV IR from Google Play on the phone you connect to the car. Package name com.agndreamz.avir.

Open the Play listing

The listing appears after the app is published. Until then, the link above is the address the store will use.
