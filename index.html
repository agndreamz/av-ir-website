Phone and Android Auto

Internet radio that stays with you in the car.

Discover stations by country and language, save the ones you return to, and add up to five of your own tabs, such as News or Tamil. Next and previous stay in the list you are listening to, on the phone and in the car.

Get it on Google Play How to use it

Discover

Country and language

Pick a country and the languages you want to hear. The default country and languages live in Settings, and you can switch the look between white and dark.

Your tabs

Up to five

Add a tab such as News or Tamil. Drag it into the order you want, and edit the name in Settings. Next and previous stay in that tab.

My Stations

Yours to keep

Save a station with the heart, pin the ones you want at the top, and drag the list into order. There is no account to create.

Android Auto

The same lists

Discover, Search, and My Stations are on the car screen, with your tabs under More. Scroll Discover with the arrows. Speak a search while driving, and type when the car is parked.

Download

Install AV IR from Google Play on the phone you connect to the car. Package name com.agndreamz.avir.

Open the Play listing

The listing appears after the app is published. Until then, the link above is the address the store will use.
