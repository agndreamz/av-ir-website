Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

AV IR is an internet radio player for phones and Android Auto. The app does not require an account. This page is the policy linked from the app and from the Google Play listing.

Search text

When you search by typing or by voice, the words you searched for are sent to the Radio Browser directory at radio-browser.info so AV IR can list matching stations. Radio Browser is an independent community service. AV IR does not attach your name, advertising ID, or an account to that request, and it does not run its own server for search.

Voice

Voice search uses the speech recognizer on your device. The recognizer turns speech into text and gives that text to AV IR. The app does not record, store, or upload the audio. The speech service you have enabled, typically Google, processes the audio under its own terms.

Playback

When you play a station, the app connects to the stream address published by that station and asks Radio Browser to count the play. Audio is played on your device. It is not uploaded to AV IR. Some stations publish unencrypted HTTP streams. Lookups to the station directory use HTTPS.

Saved stations and tabs

Favorites, recently played stations, and the tabs you create are stored on your device. A tab keeps the name you give it and the language or search you attached to it. If Android backup is turned on, that information can be included in your device backup. You can remove a favorite or a tab in the app. When a tab looks up stations, that language or search is sent to Radio Browser in the same way as a search.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Android shows playback controls. AV IR does not use that permission for marketing.

What AV IR does not do

AV IR has no accounts, no ads, and no advertising ID. It does not include analytics or a crash-reporting SDK. It does not sell personal information. It does not ask for your location.

Children

AV IR is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13.

Third parties

Radio Browser provides the station directory. The station you choose serves the audio. Google Play delivers the app. The device speech recognizer handles voice search. Each of those services has its own terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page and in the app.

Contact

Questions and requests: github.com/agndreamz/av-ir-website/issues.
